---
title: Why I keep shipping offline-first
date: 2026-03-20
---

Most “AI era” software wants your files in someone else’s data center by default. I build the opposite.

**Document Studio** and **OpenCanvasX** are local-first on purpose: merge, encrypt, OCR, design, and edit without a mandatory account or upload. That constraint forces better product decisions — clearer UX, honest performance, and privacy you can explain in one sentence.

### What offline-first actually means

Offline doesn’t mean isolated forever. It means the **default path doesn’t leak**. Optional sync, BYOK, and peer collaboration can still exist — but they stay opt-in, and the core product remains useful when the network is gone.

On desktop that shows up as packaging work, native toolchain glue, and pipelines that stay fast when every byte is local. Those are unglamorous problems. They’re also the ones that make “privacy” real.

### Why I care

If you’re shipping tools people trust with documents, start offline. Everything else is a feature you earn.
