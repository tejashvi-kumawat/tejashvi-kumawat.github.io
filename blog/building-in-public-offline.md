---
title: Why I keep shipping offline-first
date: 2026-03-20
---

Most “AI era” software wants your files in someone else’s data center. I build the opposite.

**Document Studio** and **OpenCanvasX** are local-first on purpose: merge, encrypt, OCR, design, and edit without a mandatory account or upload. That constraint forces better product decisions — clearer UX, honest performance, and privacy you can explain in one sentence.

Offline doesn’t mean isolated forever. It means the default path doesn’t leak. Optional sync, BYOK, and peer collaboration can still exist — but they stay opt-in.

If you’re shipping tools people trust with documents, start offline. Everything else is a feature you earn.
