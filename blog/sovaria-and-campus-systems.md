A portfolio full of every repo is noise. Here’s what I’m actually prioritizing — and why the order matters.

### Sovaria

My startup. Platform pieces — gateway, engines, SDK, CLI, contracts, orchestrator — live under the private [sovaria](https://github.com/sovaria) org. The public face is [sovaria.in](https://sovaria.in). The thesis is simple: modern products shouldn’t need a Frankenstein of half-wired services to get from prototype to production.

### Agents & optimization

I’m spending research energy on **AI agents** that plan, call tools, and fail gracefully — plus the optimization craft that shows up in both simulation (GeoPeriX-class workloads) and everyday backend latency. Docs for GeoPeriX live at [geoperix.readthedocs.io](https://geoperix.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

### Campus systems that ship

NSS / Himalayan Vidyadaan education tooling, DoEP frontend work, and Tryst fest ops. Not everything is open source — and that’s fine when the users are real students and organizers.

Open source still matters (Document Studio, OpenCanvasX). It just isn’t the only signal.
