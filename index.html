About me

I’m a student at IIT Delhi who ships across the stack — desktop apps, web platforms, and performance-minded backends. I care about privacy-preserving software, clean product craft, and systems that stay useful without forcing everything into the cloud.

Outside class you’ll find me building Sovaria, hardening tools like Document Studio, and working on optimization / simulation problems in the GeoPeriX ecosystem (peridynamics for geomechanics) with research partners.
