About me

I’m a 4th-year student at IIT Delhi (AIR 1834, JEE Advanced), based between Delhi and Jaipur. I work at the intersection of software engineering, systems design, and entrepreneurship — shipping products people actually use, not just demos.

Right now I’m Founder of Sovaria, building a single backend platform focused on speed, clarity, and developer experience — from gateway and engines to SDK, CLI, and orchestration. In parallel I’m Lead Software Engineer at Sprix Manabie Education, after earlier work there as a tutor (Maths & Physics for JEE aspirants) and contributing to curriculum and product engineering.

I’ve also interned in AI / ML at Wayspire (Python, regression, neural nets, Azure), mentored students with Physics Wallah, and led technical & campus roles across NSS IIT Delhi (Technical Secretary), Himalayan Vidya Daan Trust (Technical Lead), TRYST, Rendezvous, Blockchain Society, and eDC IIT Delhi. On the research side I contribute to GeoPeriX optimization / geomechanics simulation work with Optinqo & partners.

Outside org work I ship open products like Document Studio and OpenCanvasX — offline-first by design. More on LinkedIn.
