About me

I’m a fourth-year engineering student at IIT Delhi (JEE Advanced AIR 1834), based between Delhi and Jaipur. Most of my time goes into building systems that have to survive real load, real users, and real constraints — not slide decks. I care about clean contracts between services, predictable performance, and interfaces that stay honest when networks fail or privacy matters.

As Founder of Sovaria, I’m building a unified backend platform meant to replace the usual patchwork of gateways, workers, and glue code. The stack spans request routing, execution engines, typed contracts, SDK / CLI surfaces, and orchestration — with a bias toward developer experience and long-term operability from prototype to production. Public face: sovaria.in.

In parallel I’m Lead Software Engineer at Sprix Manabie Education, after earlier work there tutoring Mathematics and Physics for JEE aspirants and helping refine curriculum and product engineering for AI-assisted learning systems. Before that I completed an AI / ML internship at Wayspire (Python, regression, neural networks, NumPy / Pandas / Matplotlib, Azure), mentored hundreds of students with Physics Wallah, and held technical leadership roles across campus — Technical Secretary at NSS IIT Delhi, Technical Lead at Himalayan Vidya Daan Trust, and systems work for TRYST, Rendezvous, Blockchain Society, and eDC.

On the research / HPC side I contribute to GeoPeriX — high-performance peridynamics for geomechanics — focusing on optimization paths, tooling, and engineering workflows with Optinqo / Smart Geotechnics partners. Separately I ship open products like Document Studio and OpenCanvasX: local-first desktop software where documents and canvases stay on-device by default. Full timeline on LinkedIn.

02 · Desktop systems

Document Studio

A cross-platform offline PDF workspace (Windows / macOS / Linux) built with Flutter: merge & split pipelines, encryption, OCR, Office conversion, markup, and digital signatures — without uploading files to a third-party cloud. Distributed via a Homebrew tap and packaged for desktop installers. The hard problems are packaging, native toolchain glue, and keeping UX fast when every operation is local.

Interests

Where I’m spending intellectual energy right now — research threads and engineering bets.

AI agents & tool-using systems

I’m researching how agentic systems plan, call tools, maintain memory, and recover from failure without becoming brittle wrappers around a single model call. That includes multi-step workflows, evaluation harnesses, retrieval / grounding, and the boring but decisive parts: timeouts, retries, permission boundaries, and observability when an agent acts on real software instead of a chat transcript.

Optimization & numerical workloads

From GeoPeriX-style simulation loops to everyday backend latency, I’m drawn to optimization as a craft — profiling hot paths, reducing constant factors, choosing better data layouts, and knowing when to trade exactness for speed. I like problems where algorithms and systems meet: solvers, batching, caching, and making scientific or product code actually finish on deadline.

Platform & systems design

Through Sovaria I’m obsessed with the “one coherent backend” problem: clear contracts, gateway semantics, execution isolation, SDK ergonomics, and orchestration that doesn’t punish early-stage teams. I care about APIs that age well, CLI workflows developers actually use, and infrastructure that stays understandable when the team is small.

Local-first & privacy-preserving clients

Document Studio and OpenCanvasX come from a simple belief: useful software shouldn’t require uploading your files to stay functional. I’m interested in offline pipelines, local AI / BYOK patterns, and desktop packaging — the unglamorous engineering that makes privacy a default rather than a marketing slide.

Applied ML in education products

Sprix, Wayspire, and campus teaching work keep me close to edtech: adaptive learning loops, curriculum tooling, and the systems that sit behind tutors and students. I’m less interested in demos than in models and product surfaces that improve learning outcomes under messy real-world constraints.
