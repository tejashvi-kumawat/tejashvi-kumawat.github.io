About me

I’m a fourth-year engineering student at IIT Delhi (JEE Advanced AIR 1834), based between Delhi and Jaipur. Most of my time goes into building systems that have to survive real load, real users, and real constraints — not slide decks. I care about clean contracts between services, predictable performance, and interfaces that stay honest when networks fail or privacy matters.

As Founder of Sovaria, I’m building a unified backend platform meant to replace the usual patchwork of gateways, workers, and glue code. The stack spans request routing, execution engines, typed contracts, SDK / CLI surfaces, and orchestration — with a bias toward developer experience and long-term operability from prototype to production. Public face: sovaria.in.

In parallel I’m Lead Software Engineer at Sprix Manabie Education, after earlier work there tutoring Mathematics and Physics for JEE aspirants and helping refine curriculum and product engineering for AI-assisted learning systems. Before that I completed an AI / ML internship at Wayspire (Python, regression, neural networks, NumPy / Pandas / Matplotlib, Azure), mentored hundreds of students with Physics Wallah, and held technical leadership roles across campus — Technical Secretary at NSS IIT Delhi, Technical Lead at Himalayan Vidya Daan Trust, and systems work for TRYST, Rendezvous, Blockchain Society, and eDC.

I’ve also built Optinqo’s v03 agentic analytics stack — an MCP tool-calling data analyst with EDA / RCA / time-series modes — and contribute to GeoPeriX (C++17 peridynamics for geomechanics; see the docs). On campus I shipped the NSS IIT Delhi app, task managers, and a WhatsApp JS + RAG bot, plus open products like Document Studio and OpenCanvasX. Full timeline on LinkedIn.

GeoPeriX

High-performance peridynamics framework for geomechanics (docs) — optimized C++17 engine with PyGeoPeriX (pybind11). My work sits on optimization paths, tooling, and engineering workflows around that stack.

Interests

Where I’m spending intellectual energy — research threads and engineering bets.

AI agents & tool-using systems

I’m researching how agentic systems plan, call tools, maintain memory, and recover from failure — from Optinqo’s MCP analytics agent (EDA / RCA / time-series tool loops) to WhatsApp RAG bots that answer real campus queries. That includes multi-step workflows, evaluation harnesses, retrieval / grounding, and the decisive production pieces: timeouts, retries, permission boundaries, and observability when an agent acts on real software.

Optimization & numerical workloads

From GeoPeriX-style simulation loops to everyday backend latency — profiling hot paths, better data layouts, batching, caching, and knowing when to trade exactness for speed. I like problems where algorithms and systems meet and the code has to finish on deadline.

Platform & systems design

Through Sovaria: clear contracts, gateway semantics, execution isolation, SDK ergonomics, and orchestration that doesn’t punish small teams. APIs that age well and CLIs people actually keep in their muscle memory.

Local-first & privacy-preserving clients

Document Studio and OpenCanvasX — offline pipelines, local AI / BYOK, and desktop packaging so privacy is a default, not a marketing claim.

Applied ML in education products

Sprix and Wayspire keep me close to adaptive learning loops, curriculum tooling, and the systems behind tutors and students — models and product surfaces that hold up in messy reality.
